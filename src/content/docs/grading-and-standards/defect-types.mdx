# Defect types

The grading model classifies each grain and reports the defects it finds. These
measurements determine the [quality grade](/docs/grading-and-standards/quality-grades).

## The five graded defect factors

These five factors each contribute to the overall grade. Each is measured as a
percentage of the sample and compared against the PNS/BAFS 290:2025 thresholds.

- **Broken grains** — grains shorter than a full kernel. Determined by grain
  dimensions (size/length).
- **Brewers** — very small grain fragments, smaller than broken grains.
  Also determined by grain dimensions (size/length).
- **Discolored kernels** — kernels with discoloration or visible damage.
  (This factor covers what was formerly referred to separately as "damaged" — the
  two are consolidated into a single discolored/damaged category.)
- **Chalky grains** — grains with opaque, starchy white areas.
- **Red kernels** — kernels with red pigmentation.

## Count-only diagnostics

Two additional items are detected and reported as counts, but they do **not**
affect the grade calculation:

- **Foreign matter** — non-rice material in the sample.
- **Paddy** — unhusked grains found in the milled rice sample.

These are flagged for operator awareness but are excluded from the grading
thresholds.

## How defects are measured

Each of the five graded factors is reported as a percentage of the sample. The
percentages are what get compared to the PNS thresholds during grading. Foreign
matter and paddy are reported as counts only.

<Callout>
  The exact defect taxonomy follows PNS/BAFS 290:2025 — see the
  [Grading pipeline](/docs/technical-reference/grading-pipeline) reference for
  how each defect is detected.
</Callout>
