The grading model classifies each grain and reports the defects it finds. These
defect counts are what determine the [quality grade](/docs/grading-and-standards/quality-grades).

## Common defects

- **Chalky grains** — grains with opaque, starchy white areas.
- **Broken grains** — grains shorter than a full kernel.
- **Discolored / damaged grains** — grains with discoloration or visible damage.
- **Foreign matter** — non-rice material in the sample.

## How defects are measured

Each defect is reported both as a count and as a percentage of the sample. The
percentages are what get compared to the PNS thresholds during grading.

<Callout>
  The exact defect taxonomy follows PNS/BAFS 290:2025 — see the
  [Grading pipeline](/docs/technical-reference/grading-pipeline) reference for
  how each defect is detected.
</Callout>
