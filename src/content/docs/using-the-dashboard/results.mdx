# Results

The **Results** page is a reverse-chronological feed of every rice scan graded
by your devices.

## Reading the feed

Each entry shows the scan's quality grade, the device that captured it, and the
time it was graded. The newest scan is always at the top, and new results stream
in automatically as devices upload them.

## Opening a result

Select any entry to open the full result: the captured images, the assigned
quality grade, and the per-grain metrics behind it. See
[Reading the metrics](/docs/grading-and-standards/quality-grades) for what each
number means.

<Callout>
  Results are read-only — grades are produced by the AI pipeline and cannot be
  edited from the dashboard.
</Callout>
