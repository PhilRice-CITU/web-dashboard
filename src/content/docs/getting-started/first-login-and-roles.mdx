## Signing in

hum.ai uses email-and-password authentication. Open the dashboard, enter your
credentials on the login screen, and you land on the **Results** page.

## Roles

hum.ai has two dashboard roles:

- **Admin** — full access, scoped to a single assigned region.
- **Superadmin** — full access across all regions.

## Regions

Every device, scan, and result belongs to a region. Admins only ever see data
for their own region; superadmins can switch between regions. If you need access
to a different region, contact a superadmin.

<Callout>
  Roles and region assignments are managed by a superadmin — they cannot be
  changed from your own account settings.
</Callout>
